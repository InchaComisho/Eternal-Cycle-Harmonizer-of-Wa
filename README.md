## 🌍 What is Eternal Cycle?

**Eternal Cycle — Harmonizer of Wa —** is a civilization-tuning action RPG built around one central question:

> **How can a civilization survive without collapsing?**

This is not a game about defeating evil.  
It is a game about:

- balancing civilizations
- restoring ecological circulation
- preventing systemic collapse
- understanding the consequences of ideological extremes
- maintaining long-term harmony between nature, technology, and society

The player is not a hero or conqueror.  
The player is:

> **A Harmonizer of Civilization**

A being who intervenes in regions suffering from:

- war
- environmental collapse
- pollution
- ecosystem disruption
- ideological extremism
- circulation failure
- Void corruption

while attempting to guide the world toward sustainable coexistence.

---

## 🔁 Core Gameplay Loop

The core gameplay cycle is:

1. Observe the world map
2. Select unstable regions
3. Eliminate corrupted enemies
4. Investigate the source of collapse
5. Defeat the regional boss
6. Choose whether to **Forgive** or **Execute**
7. Watch civilizations evolve or collapse
8. Prevent the emergence of:
   - **The Void**
   - **The Eternal**

Each decision changes:

- diplomacy
- ecology
- civilization growth
- social stability
- trade
- technological progress
- long-term planetary balance

## 🌌 The World

### A Planetary Civilization System

The world is not divided into simple “good” and “evil” nations.  
Every region evolves differently.

Some become:

- cooperative trade civilizations
- militarized states
- nature-centered societies
- industrial civilizations
- isolated cultures
- hybrid societies

The same species may exist across completely different political systems and philosophies.

### 🗺 World State System

The world map changes dynamically.

#### 🟢 Green Regions
- stable circulation
- ecological balance
- trade stability
- peaceful coexistence

#### 🟡 Yellow Regions
- emerging instability
- pollution
- resource shortages
- ideological tension
- ecosystem stress

#### 🔴 Red Regions
- collapse state
- war
- Void corruption
- environmental destruction
- civilization breakdown

Difficulty is not based only on enemy levels.  
It is shaped by:

- systemic instability
- pollution spread
- ecological collapse
- social disorder
- circulation failure

### 🏛 Ancient Civilization & Observatory Hub

The player operates from a neutral ancient observatory.

This ancient civilization once collapsed after losing balance between:

- technology
- ecology
- structure
- harmony

The ruins do not provide complete answers.  
Instead, they preserve:

- fragmented records
- warnings
- philosophical principles
- circulation theories
- structural blueprints
- environmental observations

Examples:

> “Closed circulation eventually decays.”  
> “Cities must not reject nature.”  
> “Structure must connect sky, water, and life.”

The player must interpret these ideas and build their own solutions.

## ⚔ Combat System

### 3-Character Real-Time Action Combat

The game uses a fast-paced action system inspired by:

- hack-and-slash ARPGs
- team-switch combat systems
- stylish combo-based action games

Only one character is directly controlled at a time.  
However:

- instant switching
- QTE support
- combo resonance
- Ultimate synchronization

allow all three characters to function as one dynamic circulation system.

### 📱 Simple Control Philosophy

Core controls:

- Basic Attack
- Dodge
- Skill
- Special Skill

When the gauge is full:

> **Ultimate Skill** becomes available.

The system is designed for both:

- PC
- mobile devices

without overwhelming button complexity.

### ⚡ Skill Structure

Each class shares the same core structure.  
Players choose one specialization type:

- **Single Target** — high efficiency / high damage
- **Area** — wide-range attacks
- **Drain** — absorption and circulation-based recovery
- **Reflection (Auto-Type)** — automatic support systems

Examples of auto-type effects include:

- auto healing
- damage reflection
- autonomous attacks
- passive support fields

This reduces excessive mechanical stress while emphasizing battlefield flow.

### 💥 BREAK System

Bosses are designed around **circulation windows**.

When a BREAK occurs:

- enemy defense collapses
- Drain efficiency increases
- Ultimate synchronization becomes possible

Without proper BREAK management, even powerful teams may collapse.

This creates a rhythm between:

- offense
- recovery
- battlefield control
- circulation maintenance

## 🌿 Species & Philosophical Alignment

Species are not “good” or “evil.”  
Each represents a civilizational tendency.

### 😈 Demons — Harmony
Long-lived and highly intelligent.  
Focused on:

- diplomacy
- coexistence
- magical civilization
- trade networks

Powerful enough to avoid unnecessary war.

### 👼 Angels — Order
Winged humanoids.  
Focused on:

- discipline
- purification
- defense
- stability

Strong order creates safety, but excessive order risks stagnation.

### 🌿 Elves — Circulation
Nature-centered long-lived species.  
Focused on:

- ecosystems
- forests
- spirit symbiosis
- natural circulation

However, they can become overly conservative and isolationist.

### 🔨 Dwarves — Structure
Masters of:

- engineering
- architecture
- infrastructure
- underground cities
- equipment creation

They build civilization itself.

### 👤 Humans — Expansion & Dualism
The fastest-growing civilization type.  
Focused on:

- science
- industrialization
- rapid progress
- technological advancement

Capable of incredible innovation, but highly prone to imbalance.

### 🐺 Beastfolk — Trade & Adaptation
Focused on:

- mobility
- logistics
- trade
- adaptability
- neutral commerce

They often connect fragmented civilizations.

## 🌑 The Void

The Void is not simply an enemy faction.  
It is the manifestation of:

- ecological collapse
- ideological extremism
- pollution
- circulation failure
- societal fragmentation
- uncontrolled expansion

> **The Void is civilization collapse itself.**

---

## 🌿 The Eternal

The Eternal is not a final boss.  
It appears when civilization fails to maintain balance.

It is:

> **a corrective force of natural order**

The Eternal does not rule.  
It does not judge.  
It does not hate.  
It simply restores broken circulation.

### ⚠ The True Failure State

The appearance of The Eternal means:

> **Civilization failed to sustain itself.**

The true ending is achieved only when:

> **The Eternal never appears.**

---

## 🔁 Time Loop System

When collapse reaches critical levels:

- causality restructures
- civilization resets
- time loops occur

However, the world is not fully reset.

The protagonist retains memory.  
NPCs, civilizations, and species retain:

- fragments
- echoes
- emotional residue
- subconscious familiarity

Examples:

> “I feel like I’ve known you before.”  
> “This city feels familiar somehow.”  
> “I dreamed about this place.”

The world itself learns through repetition.

---

## 🌍 Natural Complement Science

Advanced civilizations eventually discover:

> **Natural Complement Science**

Instead of dominating nature, civilizations learn to support natural systems.

Examples include:

- ultrasonic mist cooling
- ocean circulation support
- deep-sea aeration
- desert greening
- microorganism restoration
- ecological circulation architecture

These are not treated as “magic fixes.”  
They are:

> **scaled extensions of natural phenomena**

---

## 🌌 Final Civilization Stage

The highest civilization stage becomes:

> **Pyramid Circulation Cities**

These are massive self-sustaining structures integrating:

- water circulation
- food production
- energy systems
- climate stabilization
- ecosystem restoration
- logistics
- ecological balance

The city itself becomes a living ecosystem.

## ⚖ The Six Principles

The world is governed by:

- Natural Law
- Harmony
- Circulation
- Structure
- Order
- Wa (Integrated Coexistence)

These principles are not morality systems.  
They are civilization stability systems.

Excessive imbalance toward any one principle eventually creates collapse.

- the consequences of imbalance

- the danger of stagnation
- the fragility of civilization
- the importance of circulation

through gameplay itself.
